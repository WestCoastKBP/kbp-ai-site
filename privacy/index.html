Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

West Coast KBP ("we", "us") is a general construction company working in Northern California. This policy explains what we collect when you ask us about a project, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

When you request an estimate through our website, a Facebook or Instagram form, a phone call, a text or an email, we collect what you give us: your name, phone number, email, the address or ZIP code of the property, a description of the job, photos you choose to send, and your answers to our questions (for example, whether you own the home and when you want to start). If you reach us through a Facebook or Instagram ad, Meta passes us the answers you submit in that form.

How we use it

We use your information to contact you about your project, schedule a visit, prepare a written estimate, do the work if you hire us, and keep records of our conversations and agreements. We also count how many requests each ad brings in, so we can see which ads are useful.

How we contact you

By submitting a request you agree that we may call, text or email you about your project. Message and data rates may apply. You can tell us to stop at any time: reply STOP to a text, or ask us by phone or email.

What we share

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising by others. We share it only with people and services that help us do the work (for example, our crew, subcontractors on your job, and the software we use for email, scheduling and records), and when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep project records for as long as we need them for the job, warranties, taxes and legal obligations. Requests that do not become a project are deleted within 24 months.

Your California rights

If you live in California, you can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We will not treat you differently for asking. Send your request to owner@westcoastkbp.com and we will answer within 45 days.

Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
