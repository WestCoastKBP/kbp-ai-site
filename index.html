AI can help write a reply. Keeping the customer's request moving still takes work. We're building KBP AI to keep the messages, decisions and next steps of that work together. Our first focus is independent professionals and small-business owners who coordinate recurring customer work across their tools.

Start with one customer request.

A request arrives by email. It becomes an ongoing matter, with its source and next step kept together, so you can come back to it later. Our current private test covers receiving the message, creating the matter and reopening it. Preparing a reply and checking what happens after it is sent are the next parts to demonstrate.

What the private test covers

One incoming work message becomes a matter that keeps its source. When the matter is reopened, its stage and next step are still there.

A system you can develop around your work.

The longer-term aim is to help people add useful workflows, tools and products as their needs grow. The customer-inquiry workflow is our first test. It is not a released builder, and it is not the limit of what KBP AI may become.

Understand the work. Choose the next step.

The product is being designed to show what has happened, what is waiting and what needs your decision. Personal and professional information must keep their own permissions. These are requirements we are building and testing.
