Keep the matter, not just the chat.

KBP AI is being built for people who already use AI but still move messages, documents and follow-up between separate tools. The aim is to keep the relevant context and next decision together.

From an incoming message to a decision

A meeting invitation needs a reply. The intended workflow brings the message into its workspace, prepares a response and waits for your approval. After sending, it should show the actual delivery result.

Connections tells you which tools can be used. Inbox brings incoming messages and decisions needing your attention into one place.

Your tools. Your boundaries.

Separate workspaces

Personal, Engineering and Business/Work are intended to keep their own context and permissions. Projects, roles and connections belong to the workspace where you need them.

Decisions stay with you

The product direction is to make proposed actions, waiting states and results visible. A connected account alone is not evidence that a complete workflow works.

These are product design goals. Signing in to a model provider does not automatically grant access to its APIs or to your other accounts.
