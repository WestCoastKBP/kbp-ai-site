# KBP AI — Knowledge Becomes Progress

KBP AI is a workspace in development for turning knowledge and AI into useful actions and checked results in real life. You keep control of decisions, data and permissions.

**Prototype in development.** This repository contains the public website, not the assistant engine. A public installer and reproducible end-to-end product demo are not available yet.

## Start here

- [Website](https://kbp-ai.com/): product direction and current status.
- [Telegram](https://t.me/kbp_ai): practical experiments and development updates.
- [hello@kbp-ai.com](mailto:hello@kbp-ai.com): our primary contact for questions, task ideas and collaboration.

Tell us what you are trying to do, where it gets stuck and the result you need. Please leave credentials, private correspondence and personal documents out of public discussions.
